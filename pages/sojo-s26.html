Case Traceability

The case traceability project had the goal of individually applying an identifier to every case that is processed on a line, so that when it leaves the facility each case can be backtraced through the packaging system back to the original input manufacturer. My contributions included:

  • Designing the entirety of the mechanical packaging, creating a completed 3D assembly of the project, while comparing several material suppliers to minimize costs.
  • Mapping the layout of the electrical cabinet attached to the assembly in CAD and adjusting to ensure fitment while minimizing footprint.
  • Building the entire electrical cabinet under supervision from the lead cabinet assembler.

When I left the internship, the project was nearly complete, and had entered the stage where they'd begun testing code. There was a hardware issue with a component beyond our control that prevented it from being completed the week that I left, but overall the project was successfully completed on my end.

Project My Work
Temporary Staff Transitions Staff transitions left the responsibilities of the only on-site mechanical engineer open for a few weeks, and during that time I attempted to fill those responsibilities by helping to overhaul some of the projects they were working on. The primary project from this period involved learning sheet metal modeling from scratch, overhauling an entire sheet metal assembly that had been spec'd too thin, and redesigning the entire model in an 11 hour shift on my final day.
Adjustable Box Mandrels Created three unique adjustable mandrel designs for trayformers, in addition to two generic parametric designs to easily create static mandrel technical drawings once I left. I created video instructions for how to change those parametric mandrels to the desired dimensions for future use after I left.
6 Axis Robotic Arms Continual management of 6 axis robotic arms used in palletizing inputted trays, as well as dismantling for transport roboticized lines at their previous facilities.
Replacement Parts Created CAD models and technical drawings for replacement parts needed to get pallet positioners and robotic end effectors back operational.

These are the most prominent projects I can remember, but there were other small tasks I did throughout as well.
