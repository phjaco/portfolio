Clock Geartrain

The clock geartrain was a project assigned in one of our courses that involved solving loading equations based on an inputted torque and weight of a clock's hands to determine the minimum acceptable values in designing a clock geartrain, and then creating the drawings for it and sourcing additional parts rated to or above the values we determined. This boiled out to solving the alternating and constant bending forces on two shafts as the hands rotate, finding the bearing stresses acting on the points along the shaft that we placed them, and creating gears that both don't break under the load and have a functional ratio.

This project required ~40 hours of work from me, 18 of which were continuous on the night of the deadline. I was responsible for all 3D models and technical drawings, which changed constantly as last minute tweaks were made, so the entire assembly and all technical drawings had to be made on the final day. The total report was 52 pages long and I was directly responsible for half of them, as well as reviewed and edited the rest to check both the work and wording.

The report is not perfect, and AI was used to create scripts that captured the MathScript from our work in Desmos and translated it into LateX to be compiled in Overwolf. Then the complete collection of pdfs was combined using a pdf combiner that was also an AI generated tool. This was disclosed to our instructor and no AI was used in reaching our actual conclusions. The reason we did this was because our group had a slight contribution imbalance such that three of the four categories of calculations were still being worked on the day prior to the due date, with a call lasting several hours between I and another groupmate to work out the final numbers. The two of us coordinated our schedules such that when they fully finalized the numbers I would wake up within the hour to begin the CAD and technical drawings. The LateX calculations were edited quickly due to this crunch, so they lack well defined variables and weren't compressed down or simplified, only labeled and numbered accordingly.

Given the larger workload required by this project and the time constraints, this is one of the course projects I am proudest of despite its flaws.
