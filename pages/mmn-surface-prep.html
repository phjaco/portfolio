Macro, Micro, Nano Material Surfaces

I enrolled in a Nanomanufacturing course that was primarily a graduate student course that had been double listed as a high level elective for undergraduate students. As part of the course, we were required to write a research paper on a topic of our choosing related to nanomanufacturing. I chose MMN surface preparation techniques for titanium implants as my brother had co-authored a few articles related to their performance so I had some amount of prior knowledge, and it also allowed me to reference my brother's work, which I thought was cool. The paper is really just a broad overview in layman's terms of several different preparation techniques and what they might change that would help create a more biomemetic structure or increase hydrophilicty for the initial stages of osseointegration. I didn't address every technique there is because I had a time limit on the project and for each technique I chose to include I spent several hours just reviewing the method first to make sure I understood it and then several more finding research papers to reference and writing the section. Even then, this paper might have mistakes that someone with a more experienced eye than mine could discern. Regardless, the complete paper is below, and hopefully demonstrates some amount of understanding regarding the different techniques.
