FRC '23

This robotics season was a refinement of the same principles we used in the '22 season. The game this year was simpler in concept, so the design metas were less open for innovation. Conceptually it required a vertical translation of a controllable end effector, so we created an elevator using aluminum extrusion at an angle conducive to the game and used a mirrored fourbar for the end effector, with an additional four bar linkage system for controlling its position. I'm unsure if it was the game or our team's improvements, but this robot required much less maintainence bar one or two extreme failures that it continued to run in spite of anyway. From a product standpoint, this robot was an excellent product, but since there was so much less hectic design and on the spot fixing, this season didn't contain the same excitement as '22.

>

My Contribution

My primary focus was on refining out the end effector's accuracy, to the chagrin of some team members. The clamping mechanism we used I believe was in the tenth major design restructure, following roughly 9 fine adjustments to narrow it out. I further developed an end effector down to thousandths of an inch in the CAD for its predicted range, but we never created it because we didn't need that accuracy. Unfortunately that model has likely been lost to time. Beyond that it was the same processes as the previous year; designing a geometric blank, applying a load, narrowing the shape of the part down based on the way stress flowed, and producing the parts. I believe we broke two endmills over the course of the season, which was a significant improvement from the prior year.

We were the third pick of the third alliance at the World championship, managing to win our division and move to the final round. Our alliance placed somewhere between 4th and 8th overall, which would place our robot individually in the 16th-32nd range. Using one of the common statistical models, we were ~123 in the world in actual play of 3,290 teams, roughly twice as strong as the prior year in which we were statistically 251st of 3,062 teams.
